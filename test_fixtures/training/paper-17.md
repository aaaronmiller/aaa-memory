# Attention Mechanisms in Transformer Architectures

## Abstract
This paper presents a novel approach to attention mechanisms in transformer architectures.
Our method achieves state-of-the-art results on the GLUE benchmark.

## Introduction
Recent advances in natural language processing have been driven by transformer models.
However, the quadratic complexity of self-attention remains a challenge.

## Related Work
Vaswani et al. (2017) introduced the transformer architecture.
Brown et al. (2020) showed scaling laws for language models.

## Methodology
We propose a sparse attention mechanism that reduces complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n).

## Results
Our method achieves 92.3% accuracy on GLUE, outperforming baselines.
